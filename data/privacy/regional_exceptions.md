# Regional Privacy Exceptions

## Purpose

TechCorp's baseline privacy program is built around GDPR (see the GDPR Compliance Summary). Several jurisdictions impose additional or different requirements. This document records the regional variations that employees handling customer data or privacy requests must apply. Where a regional rule is stricter than the baseline, the regional rule wins for customers in that region.

## California (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA. TechCorp applies the following on top of the GDPR baseline:

- **Right to opt out of sale or sharing:** TechCorp honors opt-out-of-sale requests **within 15 business days** of receipt. The "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link on the website footer and Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals are both treated as valid opt-out mechanisms; GPC signals are applied automatically for the browser session and associated account.
- **Right to know and delete:** handled through the same intake and verification pipeline as GDPR requests, with the same 30-day completion target described in the Data Deletion Process.
- **Non-discrimination:** exercising CCPA rights never affects pricing, service level, or support quality.
- California requests do not require the requester to prove residency up front; geolocation and account address are sufficient for initial routing.

## Brazil (LGPD)

For customers in Brazil, TechCorp applies the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados. Operationally, **LGPD rights are treated as a mirror of GDPR rights**: access, rectification (correction), erasure (deletion), portability, and objection are all available through the same privacy request form and follow the same verification and completion timelines as GDPR requests. Two Brazil-specific notes:

- Responses to LGPD requests are provided in Portuguese when the account language is set to Portuguese.
- The Data Protection Officer serves as the LGPD "encarregado"; regulator correspondence from the ANPD routes to the DPO the same business day, exactly as EU regulator mail does.

## Data Residency

**Personal data of EU customers is stored in the EU region** of our cloud infrastructure. Specifically:

- Primary databases, backups, and analytics stores holding EU customer data run in EU data centers.
- Cross-border transfers out of the EU occur only where a lawful transfer mechanism is in place (adequacy decision or Standard Contractual Clauses), and only for narrowly scoped vendor functions approved by Legal.
- Support agents outside the EU access EU customer records through access-controlled tooling rather than data replication; no bulk EU data is copied to non-EU systems.

Customers in other regions are served from the geographically closest supported region, but no equivalent residency guarantee is currently offered outside the EU.

## Other Jurisdictions

Countries not listed here follow the GDPR-aligned baseline. New regional requirements are assessed by Legal, and this document is updated before enforcement dates. If you receive a privacy request citing a law you do not recognize, do not guess — forward it to the privacy team, which will classify it within 72 hours per the standard verification window.
