# GDPR Compliance Summary

## Purpose

This document summarizes how TechCorp complies with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) with respect to customer personal data. It is written for employees who handle customer data or customer questions; it is not legal advice and does not replace the full internal Data Protection Standard maintained by Legal.

## TechCorp's Role

For personal data of our customers — account details, order history, support interactions, and product telemetry tied to an account — **TechCorp acts as the data controller**. We determine the purposes and means of processing. Where we use vendors (payment processors, cloud hosting, email delivery), those vendors act as processors under data-processing agreements that Legal maintains and reviews annually.

## Lawful Bases for Processing

TechCorp relies on two lawful bases for the bulk of customer data processing:

1. **Contract** (Article 6(1)(b)) — processing necessary to deliver what the customer bought: account management, order fulfillment, payment, shipping, warranty service, and support.
2. **Legitimate interest** (Article 6(1)(f)) — fraud prevention, service security, product improvement analytics, and first-party marketing to existing customers, each supported by a documented legitimate-interest assessment.

Consent is used only where required — for example, non-essential cookies and third-party marketing — and is captured and withdrawable through the customer preference center.

## Rights of EU Customers

EU customers may exercise the following rights, free of charge, through the privacy request form on our website or via support:

- **Access** — receive a copy of the personal data we hold about them.
- **Rectification** — have inaccurate data corrected.
- **Erasure** — have their data deleted (see the Data Deletion Process for timelines and exceptions).
- **Portability** — receive their data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Objection** — object to processing based on legitimate interest, including direct marketing, which we honor without requiring justification.

Requests are acknowledged promptly and completed within the statutory one-month window, extendable only in complex cases with notice to the customer. Identity verification steps are described in the Data Deletion Process document.

## Data Protection Officer

TechCorp has appointed a **Data Protection Officer (DPO)**. Employees and customers can reach the DPO **through the internal DPO contact form** on the privacy intranet page (employees) or the privacy section of the website (customers). Do not attempt to answer regulator correspondence yourself — route it to the DPO the same business day.

## Data Protection Impact Assessments

A **DPIA is required before any new processing of sensitive data** (special-category data, large-scale profiling, or systematic monitoring) begins. Project owners must submit the DPIA template to the DPO during design, not after launch. Projects without a completed DPIA where one is required will not receive production data access.
