# New Employee IT Setup FAQ

## How do I get my laptop?

Your company laptop is shipped to arrive on or before your first day. It comes pre-enrolled in device management with disk encryption enabled. If it has not arrived by 09:00 on day one, contact the IT Service Desk and a loaner will be arranged within 4 business hours.

## How do I set up my accounts?

On your first login you will be prompted to set a password (minimum 14 characters) and enroll in multi-factor authentication. MFA enrollment is mandatory and must be completed within 24 hours of first login; accounts without MFA are automatically suspended after that window.

## What software can I install?

Anything in the Self-Service Catalog installs without a ticket. Software outside the catalog requires an IT request and a license check, which typically completes within 3 business days.

## How do I connect to the VPN?

The corporate VPN client is pre-installed. Sign in with your company credentials and approve the MFA prompt. VPN is required for all access to internal systems when working outside a TechCorp office, per the Remote Work Policy.

## Who do I contact for help?

The IT Service Desk operates 06:00-20:00 local time on business days, with an on-call rotation for priority-1 outages. Standard tickets receive a first response within 8 business hours; priority-1 issues within 30 minutes.

## What about my personal devices?

Personal devices may access email and calendar only through the managed mobile app with a device PIN. Full corporate access from personal devices is not permitted — see the Equipment Use Policy for the rules on company data and personal hardware.
