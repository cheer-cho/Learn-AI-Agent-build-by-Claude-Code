# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose

TechCorp reimburses employees for reasonable, pre-approved expenses incurred while conducting company business. This policy explains what qualifies, spending limits, and how to submit claims through the expense system.

## Eligible Expenses

Employees may claim reimbursement for business travel (flights, trains, ground transport), lodging up to $250 per night in standard markets, client meals up to $75 per person, and conference registration fees approved in advance by their manager. Receipts are required for any expense over $25.

## Submission Deadlines

Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense date. Claims older than 60 days require director approval and are reimbursed only in exceptional circumstances. Approved claims are paid out with the next payroll cycle, typically within 10 business days of approval.

## Corporate Cards

Employees who travel more than 4 times per year may request a corporate card. Corporate card transactions still require receipts and are reconciled monthly. Personal purchases on corporate cards are prohibited and must be repaid within 14 days if they occur accidentally.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

The following are never reimbursable: traffic fines, minibar charges, in-flight entertainment upgrades, expenses for accompanying family members, and any expense lacking a legitimate business purpose. Questions about edge cases should be directed to the Finance team before the expense is incurred.

## Compliance

Falsified expense claims are treated as a serious disciplinary matter. Finance performs random audits of 5% of claims each quarter.
