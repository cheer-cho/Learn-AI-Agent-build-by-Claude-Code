# Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty

Every TechCorp hardware product includes a **standard warranty of 24 months** from the delivery date, at no additional cost. The standard warranty covers **manufacturing defects** — faults in materials or workmanship that cause the product to fail under normal use. Examples include a battery that stops holding charge far below its rated cycle life, a keyboard with unresponsive keys out of the box, or a display developing dead pixel clusters without impact damage.

If a covered defect is confirmed, TechCorp will, at its option, repair the product, replace it with the same or an equivalent model, or refund the purchase price. Warranty service, including return shipping for confirmed defects, is free of charge.

## What the Standard Warranty Does NOT Cover

The standard warranty explicitly excludes:

- **Water or liquid damage** of any kind, including humidity ingress.
- **Drops, impacts, and other accidental physical damage**, including cracked screens and dented enclosures.
- **Unauthorized repair or modification** — opening the device, replacing parts with non-TechCorp components, or service by a non-certified technician voids the remaining warranty for affected components.
- Cosmetic wear that does not affect function (scratches, scuffs, fading).
- Consumable parts beyond their rated life, and accessories from third parties.
- Damage from misuse, power surges outside spec, or use contrary to the product manual.

Products that arrive damaged in shipping are handled under the separate Refunds for Damaged Products policy, which is more generous — use that policy within 30 days of delivery.

## TechCare+ Extended Warranty

**TechCare+** is our optional extended protection plan, purchasable at checkout or within 60 days of delivery. TechCare+ provides:

- An **additional 12 months of coverage**, extending total protection to 36 months from delivery.
- **One accidental-damage repair** during the coverage period — this covers a single incident of the kind the standard warranty excludes, such as a drop or liquid spill. A service fee may apply depending on product category, disclosed at plan purchase.

TechCare+ does not cover loss, theft, cosmetic-only damage, or unauthorized repair, and the accidental-damage benefit cannot be used twice. Unused TechCare+ coverage transfers with the product if you sell or gift it, provided the plan is re-registered.

## Making a Warranty Claim

1. Locate your order number (format TC-XXXX) or product serial number.
2. Open a claim through the support portal, describing the fault and when it appeared.
3. Run any remote diagnostics the support agent requests.
4. If a return is needed, use the prepaid label provided; turnaround for repair is typically 7-10 business days from receipt.

Disputed claim outcomes can be escalated under the Support Escalation Procedure. Statutory consumer rights in your country apply in addition to, and are not limited by, this warranty.
