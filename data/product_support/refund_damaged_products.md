# Refunds for Damaged Products

## Scope

This policy covers TechCorp products that **arrive damaged** — meaning the item was defective, broken, or physically damaged at the moment of delivery. Damage that occurs after delivery (drops, spills, wear) is handled under the Warranty Policy instead, and change-of-mind returns are handled under the standard Return Policy.

## Your Options: Refund or Replacement

If your product arrives damaged, you are entitled to your choice of:

- a **full refund**, including original shipping charges, or
- a **free replacement** of the same product, shipped at no cost with expedited handling.

Either option must be requested **within 30 days of the delivery date** shown in your order tracking. Claims submitted after the 30-day window are assessed under the Warranty Policy rather than this one.

## What We Need From You

To process a damaged-product claim, submit the following through the support portal or by replying to your order confirmation email:

1. Your order number (format TC-XXXX).
2. **Photo evidence** of the damage — at least one clear photo of the damaged item and, where relevant, the shipping packaging. Claims without photo evidence cannot be approved.
3. A short description of what is damaged or not functioning.

You do **not** need to return the damaged unit before receiving a refund or replacement. In some cases we will send a prepaid return label and ask you to send the damaged unit back within 14 days; in others we will instruct you to recycle it locally. Our support agent will confirm which applies to your claim.

## Refund Timing

Approved refunds are **processed within 5-10 business days** and are always issued **to the original payment method**. We cannot redirect refunds to a different card, account, or gift card. Depending on your bank or card issuer, the credit may take a few additional days to appear on your statement after processing.

Replacement orders are dispatched within 2 business days of approval and receive a new tracking number linked to your original order.

## Approval Thresholds and Escalation

Tier 1 support agents can approve damaged-product refunds and replacements up to $500. **Refunds exceeding $500 require Tier 2 manager approval** under the Support Escalation Procedure; this adds up to 48 hours to the decision but does not change your entitlement.

## Abuse Prevention

TechCorp reserves the right to decline claims that show a pattern of abuse, such as repeated damage claims across multiple orders or evidence inconsistencies. Declined claimants are informed of the reason and may escalate per the Support Escalation Procedure.
