# Standard Return Policy

## Scope

This policy covers voluntary returns of TechCorp products — situations where the product works as intended but you no longer want it. Products that arrive damaged are covered by the more generous Refunds for Damaged Products policy, and defects that appear later are covered by the Warranty Policy. Always check those first; they may entitle you to a better outcome.

## Return Window

You may return most TechCorp products **within 30 days of the delivery date**. The delivery date is the date recorded by the carrier in your order tracking, not the order date. After 30 days, voluntary returns are not accepted.

To start a return, use the self-service returns page with your order number (format TC-XXXX), or contact support. You will receive a prepaid return label; return shipping is free for customers in our standard shipping regions.

## Condition Requirements

- **Unopened items** in original, sealed packaging are eligible for a **full refund** of the product price.
- **Opened items** may still be returned within the 30-day window, but are subject to a **15% restocking fee**, deducted from the refund. Items must be complete — all accessories, cables, manuals, and packaging included — and free of physical damage.
- Items returned incomplete, damaged, or showing more than inspection-level use may be refused or refunded at a reduced rate, at TechCorp's assessment upon receipt.

Original outbound shipping charges are refunded only for unopened returns; for opened returns, outbound shipping is not refunded.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following cannot be returned under this policy:

- **Software licenses and digital goods, once activated or downloaded.** Unactivated license keys in unopened packaging remain returnable within the 30-day window.
- Gift cards and store credit.
- Consumables (batteries, cleaning kits) once opened.
- Products customized or engraved to order.
- Items marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase.

## Refund Processing

Once your return is received and inspected — typically within 3 business days of arrival at our returns center — the refund is issued to the original payment method. Bank processing may add a few days before it appears on your statement. You will receive email confirmation at each step: label issued, package received, inspection complete, refund issued.

## Exchanges

TechCorp does not offer direct exchanges. To swap a product for a different model or configuration, return the original under this policy and place a new order. If the original arrives damaged or defective, request a replacement under the damaged-products policy instead — replacements there are free and do not incur a restocking fee.

## Disputes

If you believe a restocking fee or refusal was applied in error, ask the support agent to escalate your case under the Support Escalation Procedure.
