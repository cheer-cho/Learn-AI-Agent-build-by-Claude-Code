# Remote Work Policy

## Purpose

TechCorp supports flexible working arrangements that balance individual productivity, team collaboration, and business needs. This policy defines how hybrid and fully-remote work operates across the company. It applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees. Contractors should refer to the terms of their engagement agreement.

## Hybrid Model

TechCorp operates a hybrid working model. Employees in office-based roles are required to work from a TechCorp office a **minimum of 2 days per week**. Teams may designate specific anchor days (for example, Tuesday and Thursday) to maximize in-person overlap; where a team has anchor days, employees are expected to align their office days accordingly.

Office attendance is tracked at the team level, not by badge audits of individuals. Managers are responsible for ensuring their teams meet the 2-day minimum on average each month.

## Fully-Remote Roles

Some roles are designated fully remote, either because of the nature of the work or the employee's location relative to our offices. **Designation of a role as fully remote requires approval from the relevant Vice President (VP).** Employees may not unilaterally convert a hybrid role to a fully-remote one. Requests should be submitted through the HR portal and include a business justification from the direct manager before VP review.

Fully-remote employees are still welcome at any TechCorp office and are encouraged to attend quarterly team on-sites.

## Core Collaboration Hours

To keep distributed teams effective, all employees — hybrid and fully remote — must be available during **core collaboration hours of 10:00 to 15:00 local time**, Monday through Friday. Outside these hours, employees may flex their schedules with manager agreement. Meetings that span multiple time zones should be scheduled within the overlapping core hours of all required attendees wherever possible.

## Home Office Stipend

TechCorp provides a **home office stipend of $500 per year** to help employees maintain a safe and productive workspace. Eligible expenses include desks, chairs, monitors, keyboards, lighting, and ergonomic accessories. The stipend is claimed through the expense system, does not roll over between calendar years, and does not cover internet or utility bills. Equipment purchased with the stipend belongs to the employee; company-issued hardware is governed separately by the Equipment Use Policy.

## Working Abroad

This policy covers remote work within your country of employment. Working remotely from another country is governed by the International Remote Work Policy and requires separate approval — do not rely on this document for cross-border arrangements.

## Compliance

Repeated failure to meet the in-office minimum or to be reachable during core collaboration hours may result in the arrangement being reviewed or revoked. Questions should be directed to your HR Business Partner.
