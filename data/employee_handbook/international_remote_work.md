# International Remote Work Policy

## Purpose

Employees occasionally wish to work from a country other than their country of employment — for example, to extend a holiday, visit family, or accompany a partner on assignment. Cross-border work creates tax, immigration, employment-law, and data-protection obligations for both the employee and TechCorp. This policy sets out when international remote work is permitted and how to request it.

## Short-Term International Remote Work (Up to 30 Days)

Employees may work remotely from another country for **up to 30 calendar days per calendar year** in total, across all trips combined. Short-term international remote work requires:

1. **Manager approval**, recorded in the HR portal before travel begins.
2. **At least 60 days advance notice** submitted through the International Work Request form, so Legal and HR can screen for immigration or sanctions issues.
3. Continued availability during your home team's core collaboration hours (see the Remote Work Policy).
4. Use of company-managed devices and the corporate VPN at all times.

The 30-day limit is counted in calendar days, including weekends and public holidays that fall within the working-abroad period.

## Stays Longer Than 30 Days

Any arrangement exceeding 30 calendar days in a year requires **joint approval from Legal and HR**, in addition to the employee's manager. Longer stays can trigger **tax residency** for the employee and create a permanent-establishment risk for TechCorp, so these requests are reviewed case by case and may be declined even where the employee is willing to bear the personal tax consequences.

For periods longer than 30 days, remote work is permitted **only from countries where TechCorp maintains a legal entity**. The current entity list is published on the Legal intranet page and reviewed quarterly. Working long-term from a country without a TechCorp entity is not permitted under any circumstances.

## Employee Responsibilities

- Verify your own visa and right-to-work status; TechCorp does not sponsor visas for voluntary remote-work travel.
- Maintain adequate health and travel insurance for the full duration of the stay.
- Notify HR immediately if your trip is extended or shortened, so the day count stays accurate.
- Comply with the Equipment Use Policy and data-handling rules; some customer data may not be accessed from certain jurisdictions.

## Non-Compliance

Working from another country without the required approvals is a policy violation and may expose the employee to personal tax liability. HR reserves the right to require an early return or to suspend remote access for the duration of an unapproved stay. Repeat violations may result in disciplinary action.
