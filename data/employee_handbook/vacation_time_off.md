# Vacation and Time Off Policy

## Purpose

Rest is part of doing good work. This policy explains how vacation, sick leave, and related time off work at TechCorp. It applies to all regular full-time employees; part-time employees accrue vacation pro rata based on contracted hours. Statutory leave entitlements in your country always apply where they exceed this policy.

## Vacation Allowance

All full-time employees receive **25 vacation days per calendar year**, accrued monthly from the start date. Vacation days are in addition to public holidays observed at your work location. New hires joining mid-year receive a pro-rated allowance for their first calendar year.

## Carryover

We want employees to actually take their vacation, so carryover is deliberately limited. **Up to 5 unused vacation days may be carried over into the next calendar year, and carried-over days must be used by March 31.** Carried-over days not used by March 31 are forfeited without compensation, except where local law requires otherwise. Days beyond the 5-day carryover cap expire on December 31.

## Requesting Vacation

Vacation requests must be submitted through the HR portal **at least 2 weeks in advance** of the first day of leave. Managers should respond to requests within 3 business days. Requests for 10 or more consecutive working days should be raised at least 6 weeks in advance to allow for coverage planning. Shorter-notice requests may be approved at the manager's discretion for personal circumstances, but approval is not guaranteed.

Where multiple team members request overlapping dates and coverage is insufficient, requests are generally prioritized in the order received.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave at TechCorp is **separate from vacation and is unlimited** — employees should never use vacation days to cover genuine illness. Notify your manager as early as possible on the first day of absence.

A **doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days**. For absences of 3 days or fewer, self-certification through the HR portal is sufficient. Extended or recurring illness is handled with support from HR and, where relevant, occupational health; extended absences may transition to the company's disability and leave-of-absence programs.

Sick leave covers the employee's own illness and medical appointments. Caring for a sick dependent is covered by separate carer's leave — see the HR portal for details.

## Other Leave

Parental leave, bereavement leave, jury duty, and voting leave are governed by their own policies and local law. None of these draw down your vacation balance.

## Departures

On termination of employment, accrued but unused vacation for the current year is paid out in the final paycheck where required by law. Negative vacation balances may be deducted from final pay where legally permitted.

Questions about balances or accrual should go to your HR Business Partner.
