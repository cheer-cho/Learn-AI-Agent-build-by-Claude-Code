# Equipment Use Policy

## Purpose

TechCorp provides employees with the hardware they need to do their jobs securely and effectively. This policy covers acceptable use, care, replacement, and return of company-issued equipment, including laptops, monitors, phones, and peripherals. It applies to all employees and contractors issued TechCorp equipment.

## Acceptable Use

Company laptops and devices are provided **for business use. Incidental personal use is allowed** — checking personal email, reading the news, or streaming music during a break is fine — provided it does not interfere with work, consume excessive resources, or violate any other TechCorp policy. Company devices remain TechCorp property at all times, and IT may manage, monitor, and remotely wipe them in line with the Acceptable Use standard.

The following are never permitted on company equipment: disabling security agents or disk encryption, installing unlicensed software, cryptocurrency mining, and storing unlawful content.

## Company Data and Personal Cloud Storage

**Company data must never be stored in personal cloud storage accounts** (personal Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud, personal OneDrive, and similar). All company files belong in TechCorp-managed systems — the corporate drive, approved SaaS tools, and managed repositories. Syncing company folders to a personal account is treated as a data-handling violation and is reported to the Security team. See the Data Retention Policy for how long company records are kept.

## Care, Loss, and Damage

Employees are expected to take reasonable care of issued equipment. Loss or theft must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours so devices can be remotely locked.

For accidental damage, TechCorp applies a two-strike replacement standard:

- **First incident:** damaged equipment is repaired or **replaced free of charge, once per rolling 24-month period**.
- **Second incident within the same 24 months:** the employee pays **50% of the replacement cost**, deducted via payroll where local law allows, or invoiced directly otherwise.

Damage caused by gross negligence or deliberate misuse falls outside this standard and is handled case by case, potentially including full cost recovery. Normal wear and tear, and manufacturer defects, are always covered at no cost to the employee.

## Refresh Cycle

Laptops are refreshed on a standard 3-year cycle. Early refresh requires manager approval and an IT ticket documenting the business need.

## Returning Equipment

When employment ends, **all company equipment must be returned within 14 days of the departure date**. IT issues prepaid shipping materials to remote employees during offboarding. Unreturned equipment after 14 days is treated as unrecovered company property: access credentials are revoked immediately at departure regardless, and the replacement value of unreturned items may be recovered where legally permitted.

## Questions

Hardware requests and repairs go through the IT Service Desk. Policy questions should be directed to IT Operations or your HR Business Partner.
